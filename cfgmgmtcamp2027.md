# Config management Camp 2027 lets gooooooooo

## One Year OpenVoxProject

* Sesstion Type: 50 minutes
* Track: Vox/Puppet

### Abstract

In the past year, the OpenVoxProject grew a lot.
We made more releases, we hardened our pipelines and we kicked off OpenVox 9 development.
At the config management camp 2026 we discussed the future direction and outlined our plans for the coming year.

What did we achieve? Which goals did we accomplish, and what are our plans for the future?

In this talk, we will look back at the past year of OpenVox development, discuss the technical and community changes we have made, and give an outlook on where the project is heading next.

### Description

We will cover the following topics:

* build and release automation
* CI/test infrastructure
* OpenVox 9 development
* OpenVox 10 development
* compatibility or migration work
* packaging/distribution
* contributions and community-driven development
